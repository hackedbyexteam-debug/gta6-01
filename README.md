SOLMAR HEAT — an open-world crime story in Solmar City. Rico Vega owes Sal Moretti $40,000 and works it off one job at a time: 12 missions in 3 acts across a dense seeded 64×64-tile city with 5 districts, 7 drivable vehicles, 5 weapons, a 5-star wanted system with pursuing police, traffic and pedestrians, shops, a taxi side job, day/night and rain, enterable buildings with furnished interiors (hideout, gunsmith, garage, clinic and four houses, each with its own rooms), and a full HUD with minimap. The camera is GTA-style: zoomed in close and turning with you, so the road ahead is always up-screen — W walks/drives toward where you face, the mouse aims, and the minimap stays north-up. Playable offline in a desktop browser from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
