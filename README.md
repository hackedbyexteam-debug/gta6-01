SOLMAR HEAT — an open-world crime story in Solmar City, in true 3D. Dario Kane owes Viktor Hale $40,000 and works it off one job at a time: 12 missions in 3 acts across a dense seeded 64×64-tile city with 5 districts, 7 drivable vehicles, 5 weapons, a 5-star wanted system with pursuing police, roadblocks and a helicopter air unit, traffic and pedestrians, shops, a taxi side job, street races, a three-person crew with abilities (Field Repair, Ghost, Slipstream), district standing, and a full HUD with minimap. The city runs a real day/night cycle — night-dominant, opening at 21:40, with golden-hour dawn and dusk and a low warm sun — and key buildings have furnished interiors you walk into (hideout, gunsmith, garage, clinic and four houses, each with its own rooms, rendered in 3D). The camera is GTA-style third-person chase: low and close, swinging onto the road ahead, pulling back with speed. Playable offline in a desktop browser from its own files (three.js r152 vendored locally, no network).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

WASD move / drive · E interact (mission / enter car / shop — the hint always names what E will do) · mouse aim, click to shoot · R reload · Q/F/C crew · V camera · Tab phone · M music · ESC pause.
